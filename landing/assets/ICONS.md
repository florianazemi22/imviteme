# Icon and illustration provenance

Kept because `docs/architecture/09-risks.md` §9.5 treats untracked asset
provenance as a real takedown risk. Every graphic on the landing page is
accounted for here.

## Pulled: Lucide

- **Source:** https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide (`icons/*.svg`, `main`)
- **Licence:** ISC — see `LUCIDE-LICENSE.txt`. Commercial use permitted;
  the copyright notice must be retained, which is what that file is for.
- **Used in `index.html`,** inlined rather than linked so the page stays a
  single self-contained file:

  | Icon | Where |
  |---|---|
  | `pen-line` | How it works, step 1 |
  | `send` | How it works, step 2 |
  | `mail-check` | How it works, step 3 |

  Paths are copied verbatim; only `stroke-width` is adjusted to sit with the
  page's heavier outline language.

## Authored here

Everything else is drawn by hand in this repository and carries no third-party
licence:

- Hero decoration — open envelope with the invitation card, a perforated
  postage stamp with two rings, and small pieces of post (sealed envelope,
  stamp, paper plane, wax seal).
- The phone mockup, the QR glyph, the theme-card rules, the favicon.

## Not used, deliberately

**No stock photography.** Photographs of identifiable people cannot be used in
advertising without a model release, and Unsplash, Pexels and Pixabay all
exclude that from their licences. If real photography is wanted later it needs
to be commissioned or bought with a release, and recorded here.
