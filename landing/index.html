Questions

The things people ask first.

Do my guests need an app or an account?

No. They tap the link, read the invitation and reply. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for, and no email address required to RSVP — which matters more than you would think for older guests.

Can I change things after I have shared the link?

Yes. Edit anything and it updates immediately for everyone who opens it — the link never changes. Replies you have already collected are kept.

If you change the date or the venue, you can message everyone who has already replied straight from the dashboard.

What happens if I do not pay?

You can build the entire invitation for free and preview it on your own phone. Payment unlocks publishing: the live link, the QR code and RSVP collection. There is no time limit on a draft and no card required to start.

How long does my invitation stay online?

Sixty days after the event on Simple, twelve months on Complete and Studio. If you want to keep it as a memento, the ten-year archive add-on is €19 and can be bought at any point, including afterwards.

Can I still send paper invitations?

Most people do both. The print pack gives you a press-ready PDF with your QR code on it, so the card stays the keepsake and the link does the counting.

What do you do with my guests' details?

We store them so you can see your replies, and nothing else. We do not sell them, we do not market to your guests, and the whole guest list is deleted on a schedule you set after the event. You can export everything as a spreadsheet at any time.
