Multi-day celebrations

Five days. One invitation. The right guests for each day.

A wedding is rarely one evening. There is a welcome dinner for people who travelled, a rehearsal for the wedding party, the ceremony, then two hundred people at the reception and a brunch the morning after. Most invitation tools make you build that as separate events, or give up and invite everybody to everything.

Imvite keeps it as one invitation with several days inside it. You decide, per day, who is invited — everyone, or only the households you tag. Each guest sees their own schedule, and replies once for all of it.

Guests are never shown what they were not invited to. They simply see their own days.

Built for how invitations actually travel

It will end up in a family group chat. That is the plan.

Invitations do not travel by email. They go into a chat, get forwarded by a proud uncle, and land in a group of forty people. Imvite is designed around that rather than against it.

  • The preview carries your names. Paste the link and the card shows your names, your date and your theme — not a grey logo.
  • A QR code for the printed cards. Print-ready, with the quiet zone and the size a church-door scan actually needs.
  • A personal link per household, with their greeting and their days already filled in.
  • Forwarding does not break it. Someone who is not in that household gets a quiet "Not you?" and can reply as themselves. You see the new name and accept it in one tap.

Questions

The things people ask first.

What happens after I pay?

Your invitation is published straight away. You get the public link, the QR code, the link preview with your names on it, and a share screen with a message already written for you. Your dashboard opens at the same moment, so you can start sending and watching replies the same evening.

How do guests RSVP?

They tap the link and answer two questions: are you coming, and how many of you. If you are having several days, they tick the ones they are invited to. Most people finish in well under a minute. They can leave a message, add the day to their calendar, and get directions.

It works on old phones and bad connections. If the page cannot run JavaScript, the form still submits. On a wedding day the RSVP must never fail.

Do guests need an app or an account?

No. No app, no sign-up, no email address required to reply. The invitation page sets no tracking cookies and loads nothing from anyone else's servers. It is a web page that opens fast, which is the whole point.

Can I edit it after I have shared it?

Yes. Change the venue, fix a spelling, move a time, add a day — the link stays the same and everyone sees the current version next time they open it. RSVPs already collected are kept. If you change the date or the place, you can message everyone who has replied.

Can I change the wording?

All of it. Every heading, label and button on your invitation can be rewritten in your own words, and nothing you write is ever changed or translated for you.

How long does my link stay online?

Basic keeps it live until 60 days after the event. Plus keeps it for 12 months. If you want to keep it for good, the ten-year archive add-on is €19 and can be bought at any time. We will never take a published invitation offline before its date, whatever else happens.

What happens to my guest list?

It is yours. Export it to CSV or Excel whenever you like, and delete it whenever you like. We do not sell it, market to it, or use it to train anything. Meal preferences and notes are purged on a schedule after the event, and you get the export first.
