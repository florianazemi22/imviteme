Questions

The things people ask first.

Do my guests need an app or an account?

No. They tap the link, read it, and reply. Nothing to install, and no email address needed to RSVP — which matters more than you would think for older guests.

Can I change things after I have shared the link?

Yes. Edit anything and it updates immediately for everyone. The link never changes and replies already collected are kept.

What happens if I do not pay?

You can build the whole invitation free and preview it on your phone. Paying unlocks publishing: the live link, the QR code, and RSVP collection. No card needed to start, and no time limit on a draft.

How long does it stay online?

Sixty days after the event on Simple, twelve months on Complete and Studio. A ten-year archive is €19 and can be added at any time, including afterwards.

Can I still send paper invitations?

Most people do both. The print pack gives you a press-ready PDF with your QR code, so the card stays the keepsake and the link does the counting.

What do you do with my guests' details?

We store them so you can see your replies, and nothing else. We do not sell them or market to your guests, and the list is deleted on a schedule you set after the event.
